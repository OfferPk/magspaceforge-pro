# ECSS-E-ST-60-30C & ECSS-E-ST-20-07C Rev. 2 Engineering Specification

## Spacecraft Magnetic Cleanliness, IGRF-13 Geomagnetic Field & Magnetic Torquer Attitude Actuation

### 1\. Executive Summary & Regulatory Authority

This specification governs the analytical modeling, budgeting, and automated verification of magnetic cleanliness and magnetic torquer (MTQ) attitude actuation for spacecraft systems. The mathematical models and normative rules implemented in **MagSpaceForge Pro v1.0.0** strictly adhere to:

* **ECSS-E-ST-60-30C**: European Cooperation for Space Standardization — *Satellite Attitude and Orbit Control System (AOCS)*, Clause 6.3 (Magnetic Actuators) and Clause 6.5 (Rate Damping & Momentum Desaturation).  
* **ECSS-E-ST-20-07C Rev. 2**: *Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC)*, Clause 5.3 (DC Magnetic Field and Dipole Verification).  
* **NASA-HDBK-4008**: *Mitigation of Magnetic Disturbance Torques and Cleanliness Practices for Spaceflight Missions*.  
* **IAGA IGRF-13**: *International Geomagnetic Reference Field 13th Generation*.

### 2\. Physical & Mathematical Formulations

#### 2.1 IGRF-13 Geomagnetic Field Approximation

In the geocentric spherical coordinate system, the primary geomagnetic field at orbit altitude \$h\$ is modeled using the dipole expansion of the scalar magnetic potential: \$\$\\vec{B}(r, \\lambda) \= B\_r \\hat{r} \+ B\_\\theta \\hat{\\theta}\$\$ Where:

* \$r \= R\_E \+ h\$, with \$R\_E \= 6371.0\\text{ km}\$ (WGS-84 volumetric mean Earth radius).  
* \$\\lambda\$ is the magnetic latitude in radians.  
* \$B\_0 \\approx 3.12 \\times 10^{-5}\\text{ T}\$ (\$31,200\\text{ nT}\$) is the equatorial surface reference field.

The orthogonal field components are: \$\$B\_r \= \-2 B\_0 \\left(\\frac{R\_E}{r}\\right)^3 \\sin\\lambda\$\$ \$\$B\_\\theta \= B\_0 \\left(\\frac{R\_E}{r}\\right)^3 \\cos\\lambda\$\$ \$\$\|\\vec{B}\| \= B\_0 \\left(\\frac{R\_E}{r}\\right)^3 \\sqrt{1 \+ 3\\sin^2\\lambda}\$\$

#### 2.2 Spacecraft Residual Magnetic Dipole & Sensor Stray Field

Every electronic box, relay, battery line, and payload component contributes to the permanent and induced magnetic dipole moment: \$\$\\vec{M}*{sc} \= \\sum*{i=1}^N \\vec{M}*{i,perm} \+ \\vec{M}*{i,ind}\$\$ The worst-case stray magnetic field \$B\_{stray}\$ induced at the fluxgate magnetometer sensor positioned on a deployable boom of length \$R\_{boom}\$ is governed by the dipole field equation: \$\$\\vec{B}*{stray}(\\vec{r}) \= \\frac{\\mu\_0}{4\\pi r^3} \\left\[ 3(\\vec{M}*{sc} \\cdot \\hat{r})\\hat{r} \- \\vec{M}*{sc} \\right\]\$\$ Along the principal boom axis (\$\\hat{r} \\parallel \\vec{M}*{sc}\$): \$\$B\_{stray,max} \= \\frac{\\mu\_0}{4\\pi R\_{boom}^3} \\cdot 2 \|\\vec{M}*{sc}\|\$\$ Normative limit per ECSS-E-ST-20-07C: \$\$\|\\vec{M}*{sc}\| \\le 0.100\\text{ A}\\cdot\\text{m}^2 \\quad \\text{and} \\quad B\_{stray,max} \\le 10.0\\text{ nT}\$\$

#### 2.3 Ferromagnetic Torquerod Core Demagnetization

For a solid ferromagnetic cylindrical core of length \$L\$, diameter \$D\$, aspect ratio \$m \= L/D\$, and intrinsic relative permeability \$\\mu\_r\$: The Osborn/Chen demagnetization factor \$N\_d\$ is: \$\$N\_d \= \\frac{4(\\ln m \- 1)}{m^2 \- 4\\ln m}\$\$ The effective rod permeability \$\\mu\_{eff}\$ accounting for internal self-demagnetizing field is: \$\$\\mu\_{eff} \= \\frac{\\mu\_r}{1 \+ N\_d(\\mu\_r \- 1)}\$\$ For high permeability materials (\$\\mu\_r \\gg 1\$), \$\\mu\_{eff} \\approx \\frac{1}{N\_d}\$.

#### 2.4 Magnetic Torquer Actuator Sizing

The magnetic dipole moment generated by an \$N\$-turn coil energized by current \$I\$ with core cross-sectional area \$A \= \\pi(D/2)^2\$ is: \$\$M\_{mtq} \= N \\cdot I \\cdot A \\cdot \\mu\_{eff} \\quad \[\\text{A}\\cdot\\text{m}^2\]\$\$ The coil electrical resistance at operating temperature \$T\$ with wire cross-sectional area \$A\_{wire}\$ is: \$\$R\_{coil}(T) \= \\rho\_{Cu}(T) \\frac{N \\cdot \\pi (D \+ 2\\delta)}{A\_{wire}}\$\$ Where \$\\rho\_{Cu}(T) \= 1.68 \\times 10^{-8} \\cdot \[1 \+ 0.00393(T \- 20^\\circ\\text{C})\]\\text{ }\\Omega\\cdot\\text{m}\$. The instantaneous electrical power dissipated is \$P\_{coil} \= V\_{bus} \\cdot I \= I^2 R\_{coil}\$.

#### 2.5 Attitude Actuation & Reaction Wheel Momentum Dumping

The instantaneous magnetic control torque \$\\vec{\\tau}*{mtq}\$ exerted on the spacecraft is: \$\$\\vec{\\tau}*{mtq} \= \\vec{M}*{mtq} \\times \\vec{B}\$\$ Because magnetic torque is strictly perpendicular to the local geomagnetic field vector \$\\vec{B}\$, 3-axis desaturation requires orbital precession and field direction modulation. The orbit-averaged desaturation torque is: \$\$\\bar{\\tau}*{desat} \= \\frac{2}{\\pi} \|\\vec{M}*{mtq}\| \\cdot \|\\vec{B}\|\$\$ The time required to desaturate reaction wheel momentum \$H*{rw}\$ is: \$\$T\_{desat} \= \\frac{H\_{rw}}{\\bar{\\tau}\_{desat}} \\le 45.0\\text{ min} \\quad (\\text{or } \\le 0.5\\text{ orbits in LEO})\$\$

#### 2.6 B-Dot Rate Detumbling Controller

Immediately following separation from the launch vehicle upper stage, spacecraft tip-off angular rates (\$\\omega\_0 \\sim 3 \- 10^\\circ/\\text{s}\$) are eliminated using the classic B-dot control law: \$\$\\vec{M}*{cmd} \= \-k*{bdot} \\dot{\\vec{B}}*{body}\$\$ With optimal rate damping gain: \$\$k*{bdot} \= \\frac{2\\pi \\bar{I}*{sc}}{T*{orbit} \|\\vec{B}\|^2}\$\$ The kinetic rotational energy damping time constant is: \$\$\\tau\_{detumble} \= \\frac{\\bar{I}*{sc}}{k*{bdot} \|\\vec{B}\|^2} \= \\frac{T\_{orbit}}{2\\pi}\$\$ To dissipate 95% of initial kinetic tip-off rate (\$3\\tau\_{detumble}\$): \$\$T\_{95%} \= 3 \\tau\_{detumble} \\approx 0.48 \\cdot T\_{orbit} \\le 2.0\\text{ orbits}\$\$
